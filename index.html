LinguaSay

Education · Language learning

Speak a new language with confidence.

LinguaSay turns speaking practice into a game. Learners talk their way through quizzes, roleplays and arcade challenges, and get instant word-by-word feedback on their pronunciation.

  • Practise English, Spanish, French, German, Italian, Korean and Arabic
  • Real-life roleplay conversations and pronunciation drills
  • Arcade games, boss battles, streaks and weekly leagues

SumBlitz

Games · Brain training

Mental math, at full speed.

SumBlitz is a fast mental-math game that sharpens your number skills in quick, satisfying rounds. Learn clever calculation tricks, beat your personal bests and climb the weekly league.

  • Speed Round, Endurance, Mistake review and Custom modes
  • Daily Gauntlet, where everyone gets the same questions
  • Step-by-step mental math tricks, leagues and friend challenges

LoveWall

Personalization · Couples

Write on your partner's wallpaper.

LoveWall turns the lock screen into a shared space for couples. Connect with a private code, then send wallpapers and heartfelt notes that appear on each other's phones.

  • Connect privately with a 6-digit invite code
  • Send wallpapers and notes straight to your partner's screen
  • A curated collection of artwork across many moods

Snap Eraser

Photography · AI photo editor

Remove any background in one tap.

Snap Eraser uses on-device AI to cut out people and products cleanly in seconds. Drop them onto a new background, add text and stickers, and export a high-resolution image.

  • Instant AI cutouts with clean, precise edges
  • Backgrounds, solid colours or your own photos
  • Ready for profile pictures, social posts and product photos

What we do

From first idea to the Play Store

We handle the whole lifecycle of an Android app, so every part of the product is built to the same standard.

Native Android development

Fast, stable apps written in Kotlin with modern Android Jetpack libraries and clean, maintainable architecture.

UI / UX design

Every screen is designed before it's built: clear flows, consistent design systems, and light and dark themes.

On-device AI features

Smart features like AI background removal and speech recognition that run on the phone itself.

Gamification & engagement

Streaks, leagues, achievements, daily challenges and reminders that help people build a habit.

Localization

Apps that feel native in many markets. Our apps support up to 15 languages, including right-to-left scripts.

Launch, analytics & growth

Play Store publishing, store listing optimization, analytics, crash monitoring, in-app purchases and ad monetization.

Our approach

A simple process, done properly

Every app goes through the same four steps, so quality never depends on luck.

01

Research

We study the market, the audience and competing apps to find what's missing.

02

Design

Every screen is designed and reviewed as a complete system before any code is written.

03

Build & test

We build native Kotlin with automated tests and check every screen on real devices.

04

Launch & improve

We publish on Google Play, measure what users do and keep shipping improvements.

About us

A small studio with a big focus on quality

CodeBot Technologies is an independent studio dedicated to Android. We don't spread ourselves across every platform. We focus on one and aim to do it really well.

Our apps help people learn languages, train their minds, express themselves creatively and stay connected with the people they love. Each one starts with a real everyday need and is refined until it feels effortless to use.
