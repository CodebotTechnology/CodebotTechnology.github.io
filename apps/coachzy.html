Coachzy

Health & Fitness · AI workout coach

Your camera counts the reps. Your coach fixes your form.

Coachzy turns your phone camera into an AI rep counter and form coach. Prop up your phone, start your set, and Coachzy counts every rep and tells you out loud how to improve your form. It works for home workouts and the gym, with no sign-up needed.

Features

A personal coach in your pocket

AI camera rep counter

Live rep counting with a skeleton overlay. Fully tuned for squats, push-ups, lunges, planks and more, with 74 extra exercises in beta.

Form check & voice coach

Spoken cues like "Go lower" and "Chest up" while you move, plus a form score after every camera workout.

Plans for home & gym

Answer a few questions and get one of 15 plans built around your goal, equipment and training days.

Gym log & plate calculator

Log every set with a rest timer, barbell plate calculator, lift history and progressive overload tips.

Learn 108 exercises

Step-by-step photos, key cues, common mistakes and motion playback for every move.

Progress & challenges

Streaks, personal records, a form score trend and weekly challenges like 100 squats this week.
