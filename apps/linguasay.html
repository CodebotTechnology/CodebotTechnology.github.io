LinguaSay

Education · Language learning

Speak a new language with confidence.

Most language apps keep you tapping. LinguaSay gets you talking. Learners practise through speaking quizzes, real-life roleplays and fast-paced arcade games, guided by Kiki the parrot, and see word-by-word feedback on how they sound.

Features

Everything you need to start speaking

Speaking-first lessons

Say it, listen and repeat, answer aloud, translate aloud and more. Every lesson gets you talking.

Word-by-word feedback

Instantly see which words you nailed, which were close and which need another try.

Real-life roleplays

Practise everyday situations, from ordering a coffee to getting around on a trip, in branching conversations.

Arcade games

Word Rain, Voice Racer, tongue twisters, karaoke shadowing, Pronunciation Bingo and more.

Rewards that keep you going

XP, combos, streaks, chests, daily quests, boss battles and a weekly league.

Stories & phrasebook

Shadow short stories line by line and keep handy phrases ready for real conversations.
