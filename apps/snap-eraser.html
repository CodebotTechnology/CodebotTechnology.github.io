Snap Eraser

Photography · AI photo editor

Remove any background in one tap.

Snap Eraser gives everyone professional-looking cutouts in seconds. On-device AI separates the subject from the background with clean edges. Then place it on a new background, add text, stickers and effects, and save it in high resolution.
