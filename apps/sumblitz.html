SumBlitz

Games · Brain training

Mental math, at full speed.

SumBlitz turns mental arithmetic into a fast, rewarding game. Race the clock in quick rounds, learn shortcuts that make hard sums easy, review your mistakes and watch your speed and accuracy climb, one round at a time.

Android 60-second rounds App in 14 languages No sign-up needed

Features

Train your brain, beat your best

Four ways to play

Speed Round against the clock, Endurance until three misses, Work on Mistakes, or a Custom round you set up yourself.

Daily Gauntlet

A fresh challenge every day, with the same questions for everyone. Race the clock and claim your spot.

Learn mental math tricks

Short step-by-step lessons, like multiplying any 2-digit number by 11 in seconds, that you can use straight away.

Weekly leagues

Earn XP from every mode and climb the league table to get promoted each week.

Challenge your friends

Share a challenge link and see who's faster on exactly the same set of questions.

Progress & achievements

Track accuracy, average answer time and streaks, and unlock achievements as you improve.
