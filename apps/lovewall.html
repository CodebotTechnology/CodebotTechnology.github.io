LoveWall

Personalization · Couples

Write on your partner's wallpaper.

LoveWall makes your lock screen something you share. Connect with your partner using a private code, then send beautiful wallpapers and little notes that show up on their phone, so every unlock is a moment to smile.

Features

Little messages, big smiles

Connect privately

Create a unique 6-digit code and share it with your partner to link your phones.

Send it to their screen

Pick a wallpaper, add your message and send it. It appears right on your partner's phone.

Write on wallpapers

Add names, quotes or a sweet note, and style it with colours, sizes, frames and alignment.

Curated artwork

Browse AI-crafted designs across moods, from cute animals and soft toys to anime and night romance.

Your story together

See the wallpapers and notes you've shared and your days together, and keep your memories in one place.

You stay in control

Choose whether your partner can change your wallpaper or write on it, and disconnect or report at any time.
